AWAKER
Keeps your Mac awake with the lid closed, so Claude Code and your long tasks keep running.

You need an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or later) on macOS 26.


INSTALL

1. Drag Awaker to the Applications folder.
2. Open Awaker from Applications.
3. macOS says "Awaker" was not opened: that's expected, the app isn't distributed through the App Store.
   Click "Done" (not "Move to Trash").
4. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to Security, and click "Open Anyway" next to Awaker.
   Confirm with your password. You only do this once.

If you prefer Terminal, this command does the same:
   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Awaker.app


FIRST USE

1. In Awaker, click "Install authorization…" and enter your administrator password, once.
   Keeping the Mac awake with the lid closed goes through the system command pmset, which needs
   administrator rights; the authorization covers that command only.
2. Click "Start session", then close the lid: the Mac stays awake.
3. Awaker's icon stays in the menu bar, to start or stop a session without opening the window.


GOOD TO KNOW

- With the lid closed the Mac no longer sleeps: don't put it in a bag during a session, it gets hot.
- Awaker stops on its own when the battery is low or the Mac overheats (see Settings).
- Auto mode (Settings) keeps the Mac awake while an agent is working, and lets it sleep 3 minutes later.
- Quitting Awaker always gives the Mac its normal sleep back.
- Awaker updates itself from Settings > Updates.
- To uninstall: in Awaker, Settings > "Remove" the authorization, then move the app to the Trash.
